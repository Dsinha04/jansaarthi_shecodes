MEMBERSHIP AND SHARE AGREEMENT
1. The society may amend these bye-laws or conditions at any time without informing the member.
2. If the member files any complaint against the society, the share capital and deposits of the member shall be forfeited.
3. The member agrees that the decision of the Board is final and binding and there shall be no appeal.
4. The society may adjust any dues against the subsidy received in the name of the member.
5. This membership renews automatically every year.
6. Sign immediately today, the offer expires today only.
