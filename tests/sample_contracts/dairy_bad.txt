MILK SUPPLY AND INPUT AGREEMENT
1. The member shall purchase all cattle feed and medicines only from the society at the prices fixed by the society.
2. The society will deduct all dues from the member's milk payment without separate consent.
3. If the member sells milk elsewhere, liquidated damages of Rs. 10,000 shall be payable.
4. Any dispute shall be referred to a sole arbitrator appointed by the society.
5. This agreement shall be automatically extended for the same period.
6. The society may change the milk rate or any condition at any time without prior intimation.
