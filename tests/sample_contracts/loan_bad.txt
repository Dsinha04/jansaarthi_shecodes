LOAN AGREEMENT
1. The borrower shall sign the loan papers on blank stamp paper and the society will fill in the amount later.
2. The rate of interest shall be as decided by the Board from time to time.
3. The society may revise the terms of this agreement at its sole discretion without notice to the borrower.
4. Penal interest at 36% per annum shall be charged on any overdue instalment.
5. Processing charges and other charges as applicable shall be levied.
6. A prepayment penalty of 5% of the outstanding amount applies if the loan is closed early.
7. The society holds a lien on all assets and land of the borrower.
8. The society may recall the entire loan immediately at any time.
9. The borrower waives all legal rights to approach any court or the Registrar.
