GUARANTEE AND SECURITY DOCUMENT
1. The guarantor is jointly and severally liable with the borrower for all dues, without any limit.
2. The borrower shall give an irrevocable power of attorney over the mortgaged land in favour of the society.
3. The borrower shall hand over three blank signed cheques to the society as security.
4. The society will retain the original title deeds of the land until all dues are cleared.
5. Interest will be compounded monthly on all overdue amounts.
