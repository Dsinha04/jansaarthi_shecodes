LOAN SANCTION LETTER
1. Loan amount: Rs. 50,000. Rate of interest: 9% per annum, fixed for the full term.
2. Repayment: 12 equal monthly instalments of Rs. 4,400 on the 5th of each month.
3. A one-time processing fee of Rs. 500 is deducted at disbursement, as shown in the fee schedule attached.
4. The loan is secured by a hypothecation of the tractor purchased with this loan only.
5. The borrower may close the loan at any time without any extra charge.
6. Any change in terms needs the written agreement of both parties.
7. Complaints may be made to the Secretary and then to the Registrar of Cooperative Societies.
