Chapter 1 Membership

Section 1. Who can become a member
Any adult resident of the area of operation may apply to become a member of the Sunrise Test Cooperative Society by paying the entry fee and buying the minimum number of shares fixed in the bye-laws.

Section 2. Leaving the society
A member may resign by giving thirty days written notice to the Secretary. Shares are refunded after all dues are cleared.

Chapter 2 Loans

Section 3. Loan application
A member may apply for a crop loan by submitting the application form, land record, identity proof and a passbook copy to the loan committee. The committee must give a written decision.

Section 4. Interest and charges
The society must show the rate of interest and every charge in writing on the loan sanction letter. No charge that is not written in the sanction letter may be recovered from the borrower.

Chapter 3 Complaints

Section 5. Complaint to the society
A member who has a grievance must first give a written complaint to the Secretary and obtain a stamped acknowledgment.

Section 6. Complaint to the Registrar
If the society does not resolve the complaint, the member may write to the Registrar of Cooperative Societies with copies of the complaint, the acknowledgment and supporting documents.
